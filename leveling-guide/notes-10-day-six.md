## The fringe (Hills/Plains) as a hunting ground, L4–L6

- Geography west of the West Gate: Field → Hill (wall-hole) →
  Hills field → Small rise → Valley → Large grassy field → Grassy
  plain, plus the stream country (Stream bank, Stream junction,
  River bank, Sloping field, Open field, Tall rise, Small
  mountain). The wall hole connects Wall Road to a second Hill
  room (N Field, W Hills).
- Prey that pays: field mice, prairie dogs, ground hogs, green
  lizards, blackbirds, European swallows. Fidos remain the
  everywhere-filler. Respawn cycles run ~30+ min — rotate wide;
  late-block XP comes from wander-ins.
- Southern Residential's Pond (route: Western Bridge → Concourse
  → Promenade → Park → Pond) holds swans and ducks. SWANS: heal
  FULLY between fights and are very hard to hit with the 0%-staves
  staff — not worth re-engaging. KICKS FAIL IN WATER at the Pond
  ("The water slows your kick down").
- Do-not-engage at L5 (consider-verified): HUGE prehistoric
  vulture (deadly verdict, non-aggressive), wild mustang, big
  horned doe, big horn ram, stray cat ("very tough battle"),
  Fluffy the baby dragon. Mountain goat and large elk both read
  "You think you could do it" — NOT clearly favorable; goat
  skipped repeatedly, elk never attempted (the full precondition
  set — full HP + fed + watered + fresh score — never coincided
  with a sighting).
- THE CROW, closed at L5: found on Main Street after a day of
  searching (it roams; Temple Square is NOT its reliable haunt).
  Consider at L5: "He is the same level as you. You would probably
  die..." Still too strong. Re-consider at L6+ still open.
- Pigeons/chipmunks: pigeons became hittable sometime in L5–L6
  play (15 courier pigeon kills, incl. the +83 L6 crossing stun)

## Night is a hard wall outside

- A lit torch does NOT clear "(too dark to tell)" exit labels for
  outdoor rooms. Fringe blocks must be planned around the ~30-min
  game day; after dark, hunt the city or Southern Residential.
- The North Gate portcullis is SHUT at night and `open portcullis`
  does not help — the city seals. (West Gate travel worked during
  the day blocks; do not count on any gate after dark.)

## The ferocious rabbit — a full day's data

Four ambush events in one session (plus a pursuit and a sleeping
attack). Refined protocol facts:

- It RESPAWNS: leg 1's rabbit was killed (audited sequence: flee
  was the first command after the marker; automatic combat rounds
  incapacitated it; the flee PANIC-failed; the downed rabbit was
  finished with `kill` per finish-what-you-start; corpse empty)
  and later legs met it again.
- It FOLLOWS a fleeing player room to room (leg 3b: four rooms of
  pursuit, HP bottomed at 15 — career low) and it ATTACKS SLEEPING
  players (Tall rise). NEVER sleep in the fringe; keep moving
  until contact breaks; regen out of contact.
- Flee-first held every time; no stand was attempted all day (the
  session's stand exception stayed unused; thirst at contacts
  would have voided it anyway).
- Operator rule confirmed workable: a FLED ambush is not an
  engagement — no country exile; but never re-enter the ambusher's
  room, and abandon that sector for the block.

## Hazards and mechanics notes

- **The Market Square manhole is a flee hazard.** Fleeing a fido
  fight in Common Square, a random flee direction went DOWN into
  "The quadruple junction under the dump" (Storm Drain,
  off-limits). Exited Up in one command. No ZONE-VIOLATION marker
  printed for the dip — the relay's zone guard did not fire on a
  flee-entry. Avoid fighting in Common Square at low HP.
- **Link-death save rollback is real XP loss.** The 13:39 reboot
  caught Testudo mid-fight (56 HP, link-dead). On reconnect the
  game had restored an earlier save: log peak 5,546 → restored
  5,269 = **277 XP lost**. Compare s97: link-dead while ASLEEP in a
  rent chamber cost 0 XP. Posture when the link dies is what
  matters — a mid-fight rollback reaches back to the last save.
- `deposit <item>` at the Bank prompts "What account number?";
  neither answering the number nor `deposit tin crown 0000-12CB`
  worked (exit leg). Rent, however, accepted the carried tin crown
  — tin crowns are NOT rent-blocking valuables in practice.
- Mortally wounded mobs can walk away and be lost (one fido, +42
  wound XP, no R.I.P.) — finish fast. Janitors clear corpses in
  ~a minute — loot instantly (one fido corpse lost to the janitor).
- Slug: sighted but no lootable corpse again today (steals caught
  mid-attempt, per the standing mechanic).
