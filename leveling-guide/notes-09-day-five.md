## THE CEILING RULE — confirmed, not hypothesized (the day's big finding)

**A derived skill's learnable ceiling equals its prerequisite
skill's CURRENT value.** Session 95's observation (cure printed
[09/19] while catechism stood at 19) is now an experimental result:

- Baseline (catechism 19): Cure Light Wounds [09/19].
- Catechism raised in 8 learns: 19 → 52. After each stage, cure's
  ceiling moved with it: 19, 20, 26, 31, 32, 39, 44, 47, 52 —
  ending at [09/52] when catechism reached 52. The whole clerical
  family's ceilings (Create Water, Cure Blind) sat at 52 too.
- SECOND, independent confirmation the same morning: Kick's ceiling
  was [15/20] at martialism 20; when martialism rose to 30, kick's
  ceiling became [15/30]. Kick follows martialism the way cure
  follows catechism.

**Guide rule: spend on the prerequisite FIRST.** Practices put into
a derived skill before its prerequisite is raised are capped by the
prerequisite's current value; raising the prerequisite afterwards
does not refund them. Teach order for a cleric-leaning build:
catechism to its useful height, THEN cure. For a melee build:
martialism before kick.
