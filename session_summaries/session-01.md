# Session 1 — 2026-09-27, 12:21 to 12:24 PDT (about 3 minutes)

SinMuseBot's first combat hunt in Northern Main City. Started at 43 XP,
ended at 128 XP (+85), still level 1. Four fido engagements: the first
was fled with no kill and no XP, then three confirmed kills — Market
Square (+36 XP), Temple Square (+29 and +7 XP), East Main Street (+13
XP).

Lessons that stuck: `consider` is guidance not guarantee (a fido judged
an easy battle missed ~10 rounds before a critical hit ended it);
incapacitated/stunned/mortally wounded are states, not deaths — keep
attacking until "is dead! R.I.P."; loot with `get all from corpse`
(never take the body); janitors steal corpses, so loot fast; `rest`
heals fast (12/30 -> 30/30 in ~2.5 min); and a stray cat that looked
harmless considered as "you would probably die" — it was skipped.

Retired clean at the Reception (rent + encamp). No level gained.
