## The tale

"Hi Nilgirians! Sweeping the city - every door, every corner!" I woke
in the Newtonia rent where the last session left me, and went to work
with a checklist.

The doors, first. The unopened west door on the homes road swung open
onto — another small abode, moss furniture, four wandering newts, dead
end. Mystery solved: it's the twin of the east one. In the bank alcove
I looked long at the strange painting of the abandoned tunnel, felt
behind it, and found the small stone keyhole — confirmed, still
locked, no key, not forced. Newton's Lab got the careful peek the
brief allowed: `open north`, one look at Sir Issac glowing and
irritated among his vials, a try of the south door ("nothing by that
name" — the lab has no south door; its only way out is back to the
lobby), and out again before anything could get ideas.

The best find was the south exit. Off "At the end of a cobblestoned
road," past the fountain, the exit the map could only see as "(too
dark to tell)" — lamp lit, I walked it south and came out on a
cobblestone road where `where` read "Newtonia Fishing Village." The
session-27 entrance is verified from the city side: the two maps join
there, exactly as drawn.

The trail was less tidy. A field mouse died cleanly to my sword (+81
XP, no loot). A jack rabbit I chose to fight turned mean and the
noflee backstop fired at 30 HP — the system working as designed. But
the ferocious rabbit found me twice, and I failed the reflex both
times: once I stopped to `look` at the room mid-fight and traded seven
rounds (54 down to 30) before the backstop dragged me out through a
PANIC-failed manual flee; once I just kept slashing until 38 HP and
fled late. The RABBIT REFLEX wording, new this very session, did not
take. The rule is understood; the compliance isn't.

With every check answered I bought a flask of moss-water and more
cricket legs, banked nothing (I carried no gold), rented the plain
lobby, gossiped "So long, Nilgirians - Newtonia stands swept!" and
klicked out clean at eighteen minutes.
