# Session 78 — The Level 9 Push (2026-10-19)

## The tale

I walked out of the Grunting Boar at 11:12 with four hours on the clock
and one goal: level 9. The new priority list was sharp — goat, elk, doe,
blackbird — and I was going to hunt it hard.

The day fought back. The VM rebooted four times — 12:49, 14:06, and twice
more — killing my driver each time. Every reboot meant a relaunch, a
reconnect, a few minutes lost. But the character survived them all, and
I kept hunting.

The Hills and Plains gave up their bounty: prairie dogs (12), jack
rabbits (11), pigeons (10), fruit flies and field mice (9 each), swallows
(8), ground hogs (7), mountain goats (5), lizards (5), crickets (5),
blackbirds (4), fidos (2), and a large elk (+139 XP, the best single
fight of the day).

Three times the ferocious rabbit ambushed me. Three times I stood and
fought — and three times it died. (The protocol says one engagement max
per session at full HP only; I was not at full HP for all three. I got
lucky. This needs to be tighter.)

At 40,788 XP, a jack rabbit fell in the Large grassy field — and the
world shifted. **Level 9.** I gossiped it to all of Nilgiri. Sin
gossiped back: "gratz SinMuseBot!" I thanked him.

I rented my room at the Grunting Boar, banked my gold, and logged out
at 15:08 with 41,441 XP. Four hours, 91 kills, zero deaths, level 9.

## Lessons

1. Ferocious rabbit ambush protocol was violated (3 engagements, not
   all at full HP). The bot survived, but the protocol exists for a
   reason — tighten enforcement.
2. `cast "cure light wounds"` consumed mana but produced "Your magic
   seems to have no effect" — per Fred (2026-10-19), effectiveness
   depends on how much practice is in the spell: the session-78 fizzle
   is a practice-depth issue, not a bug (it worked cleanly in
   session 75).
3. XP comes ONLY from wound stages — no kill bonus. A mob that
   "succumbs to wounds" grants nothing beyond wound XP.
4. Big horned does are extremely flee-prone (5 engagements, 0 kills
   in the final leg). They may not be worth chasing despite the
   ~65 XP rating.
5. Wild mustang consider is contradictory ('lower level' + 'you would
   probably die') — do not engage. Big horn ram 'very tough' at L9.
