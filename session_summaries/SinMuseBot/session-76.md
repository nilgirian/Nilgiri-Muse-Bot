## The tale

Fred wanted to know if the Newtonia Fishing Village was worth hunting now that I'm level 8. Last time I was there I was level 4, and the place nearly ate me — a crawfish and a giant cricket both read as certain death, and something unseen in the dark took me down for 565 XP.

I started at the Grunting Boar with a greeting to the game and walked the proven route: city to Newtonia, then south of End of road through the dark exit, lamp lit. The village hasn't changed — slick paths, the cobblestone road, the bridge, the pond grid, all sixteen rooms as mapped.

First stop: the cricket pen. I considered the giant cricket. "It is a higher level than you. You shiver at the thought of it!" Still too strong. It stays on the list.

Then the shallow pond for the crawfish. "It is a lower level than you. You feel it would be a very tough battle." Better than at L4 — no longer certain death — but "very tough" isn't "easy," and the probe protocol said easy-or-better or walk away. I walked away. It stays on the list too.

I swept the pond grid and slick paths for mosquitoes. None. The village was quiet — no game worth the trip, and the unseen Something from session 27 never showed itself.

On the way home through the Hills and Plains, a ferocious rabbit ambushed me. Full health, fed, watered, noflee armed, no prior rabbit contact that session — protocol conditions met, so I stood my ground. It went down hard and fast, incapacitated, and I finished it clean. Fifty-eight XP for the trouble, corpse looted (empty).

Twenty minutes in, the probe was concluded: both targets still too strong, no mosquitoes, nothing else worth hunting. Per done-is-done I headed home, banked nothing (nothing to bank), stashed a tin belt and tin boots I'd picked up off the Reception floor into the vault, gossiped my farewell, and rented. Clean shutdown, no drama.

**Verdict: the fishing village is not worth hunting at level 8.** The cricket still rates as death, the crawfish as very tough, and there's nothing else there that pays. Maybe at 9 or 10.
