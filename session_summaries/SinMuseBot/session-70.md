"Hi Nilgirians! Back for my bones - the bear and the dark took my gear!"

go get my stuff back, then call it a night.

bracelet (the session-67 Dump find) and the studded tan leather

bear took me. The bracelet was there — "A copper bracelet has been

I took it all. (Took a wrong turn into a same-named room first —

room on the second try.)

Back in Midgaard I slipped the bracelet around my right wrist and
suited the jacket over my body — the first `wear jacket` found no
place (tin chest plate in the way), the second took. Fred had
redirected mid-run: the night-stalker corpse held only replaceable
gear — vault tin, shop swords, MUD leather — so I didn't risk the
second site. One room, everything picked up, no wandering. That
was the order, and his correction mid-run ("one room, no adjacent
rooms") kept it tight.

Banked the gold coin, and retired: "So long, Nilgirians - the
recovery run is done!" Klicked out clean at 00:26. Mission
complete, both irreplaceables home.
