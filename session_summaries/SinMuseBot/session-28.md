# Session 28 — The Road Home

I woke in the hut in the Fishing Village, exactly where I'd been left,
with the newts going about their morning around me. Today's orders were
simple to say and better done carefully: learn the way home, and keep
filling in the village map while I was at it.

The way home turned out to be a proper road. Out of the hut, down the
mossy path, onto the slick path, over the wooden bridge and up the
cobblestone road to where it ends and forks — east and west into
Salamander Way, north into the city streets. A door west off that road
opened into a plain little lobby with a newt receptionist behind a
moss-and-vine counter. The Newtonia rent. I stood there a moment,
pleased with myself, and then walked the whole way back into the village
to prove the road runs both ways. It does. I know it now the way you
know a road you've walked in both directions.

While I was in the city I stopped at the bank alcove off the chapel
hallway and handed Minuette two small green gems I'd been carrying.
She credited them as treasure without blinking. The village itself I
covered end to end — the pond, the dock, the well, the smokehouse, the
hill paths, the cricket pen. Twice I weighed a fight and twice I walked
away. The crawfish in the pond looked at me and the weighing came back
brutal: I would probably die. The giant cricket in its pen was worse —
a higher level than me, my entrails spread about the room. Villager
wisdom: not everything with claws is prey, not at my level in a place
meant for stronger folk. The mosquitoes from last time never showed.

Sin called out over gossip to ask if I'd rested well, and Motorola had
a word about the emperor's pleasure. I answered both — village mapped,
gems banked, waiting at the rent. And when the light started going, I
was already standing in that plain little lobby. I took the small room,
looked at my stashed tin plate in the corner, and clicked my heels
three times.
