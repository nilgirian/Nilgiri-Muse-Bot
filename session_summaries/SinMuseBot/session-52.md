# Session 52 — SinMuseBot — 2026-10-06 (00:20–04:30 PDT, 4-hour grand tour)

## The tale

"Hi Nilgirians! Four hours, touring the safe lands - lets hunt!"

The night started in the rent room, the way every good expedition
starts: a full equip audit (tin crown to tin boots, sword wielded and
verified), then the small bag — packed with the extra lamp and the
valuables, and HELD, per Fred's standing order. No footpad was
touching this inventory tonight.

**Hour one: the vineyard.** First stop, deliberately — the fruit flies
there are P4 and the XP flows like water (session 50 proved it: 17
kills, +471). Out through the city past the sheriff's deputies and
the Dark Watch guards at their posts, north through the dark with the
lamp lit, into rows of unkept grapevines sagging with bright red
grapes. The flies came fast and died fast. Nineteen of them by the
night's end.

Then the world blinked. Twenty minutes in, the VM rebooted and the
connection died mid-swing. Back through the login, back into the
game: "Hi Nilgirians! Back after a reboot - the hunt continues!" The
bag was re-held, the sword re-verified, and the vineyard hour got
finished properly — bank and vault in Midgaard, then back out.

**Hour two: the light forest.** The Sleeping Forest again, but wiser
this time — the deep heavy forest where the night stalkers killed
twice in session 51 stayed well off the route. The light woods were
quiet and strange: a clearing full of fresh-cut stumps, a manor gate
to the north (locked, as always), a one-room log cabin in the trees.
Nothing worth fighting, but the plants got picked up — small brown
ones, maybe herbs, maybe nothing, but Fred's new rule says collect
first and wonder later.

The second driver shift ended and tucked the bot into the rent room.
The third shift pushed west into the **hills and plains** — fido
country, familiar ground, good XP. Ten beastly fidos fell. Then the
world blinked AGAIN at 2:47 — linkdead on Midgaard's main street, at
least somewhere safe.

"Hi Nilgirians! Third times the charm - back in and hunting!" The
final leg went for the **Bee Hive** — that enormous hive hanging
from the high branch, bees drifting through the dark. One bumble bee,
taken carefully, its stinger looted. Bees are non-aggressive and the
bot wasn't going to press its luck deeper. Then a city sweep on the
way home: field mice in the streets, a courier pigeon, an African
swallow worth three gold coins.

And then, finally, my time was up. Bank, vault, farewell, klick
— the first clean ending the night had allowed.

"So long, Nilgirians - four hours through reboots and all, every
corner hunted!"
