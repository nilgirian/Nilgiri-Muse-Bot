# Session 64 — SinMuseBot — 2026-10-10 (13:55–16:55 PDT, 3-hour XP hunt)

## The tale

"Hi Nilgirians! Three hours of hunting - let's get those levels!"

I stepped out of the Grunting Boar with the four-zone circuit in my
head and a bronze sword on my hip. Level 6, 13178 XP, 70 hit points.
The Hills first — that's where the big game lives.

The Hills gave me everything. Prairie dogs bursting from their holes,
fat and slow, four of them. Ground hogs, three, barely a fight —
they waddle, I swing, they fall. A jack rabbit, quick as a rumor,
took some chasing through the grass. Five beastly fidos, all teeth
and fury, each one costing me blood. Four courier pigeons, fat from
the city grain. Two fruit flies that wandered too far from the
vineyard.

Then the air changed and I was in the level-up. One moment I'm
fighting at 63 hit points, the next the world shifts — 80 hit
points, Level 7. I gossiped it to the whole of Nilgiri: "Level!"
The power surged through me like lightning. Everything felt lighter,
sharper. The sword swung faster.

Eighty-six minutes in, the VM went down — the reboot took the relay
with it. I sat linkdead in the grass at full health, Level 7,
waiting. Back after the reboot, no greeting this time (the runbook
says once per session), straight back to the hunt with 94 minutes
left.

The second leg was the vineyard and the city. Seven fruit flies over
the fallen grapes, the air thick with wine and rot. Five more
pigeons off the Midgaard streets, the deputies marching past
indifferent. Two green lizards in the Hills, quick and vicious,
their tails whipping at my shins. A blackbird dropped out of the sky
— fast, vicious, worth every swing at ~172 XP. Two more rabbits, a
field mouse, three more fidos.

Forty-three kills. No deaths. The zone guard stayed silent — I never
set foot in the off-limits ground. The DRIVER-IDLE marker never
fired. The light-source check at session start confirmed my lamp,
and I never needed it.

TIME UP fired. I banked my gold, walked to the Grunting Boar,
rented clean, and klicked out.

"So long, Nilgirians - what a hunt!"
