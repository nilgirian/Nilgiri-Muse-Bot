## The tale

"Hi Nilgirians! An hour of hunting - kick testing tonight!"

This session had a mission beyond the hunt: Fred wanted to see Kick
in action before deciding where the 14 Level-7 practices go. Kick is
manual — you type `kick` in combat, it doesn't fire on its own — and
the driver had never used it. Not once in sessions 62, 63, or 64.

First order of business: `help kick`. The help file lays it bare.
Damage = (your level + your damroll) / 2 + 1. You wait 3 rounds
afterward — that's the cooldown Fred warned about. A failed check
or fumble does no damage. And the catch: whenever a kick is made
(hit or miss), the kicker is stunned for rounds of violence. At 50%,
Kick grants +2 hits as a level-up bonus.

The hunt itself was the standard circuit. Two courier pigeons off
the city streets, fat and slow. Four fruit flies over the vineyard
grapes. A field cricket, an African swallow, two beastly fidos —
all teeth and fury. Ten kills, no deaths.

But the real story is in the kick data. Thirty-seven kick attempts
across the hour. Eight explicit "fail to follow through" messages.
Zero landed kicks that I can find in the log. At 22% skill, the
d100 roll needs to come up 22 or under just to pass the check —
then the mob still gets to dodge. The math was never in its favor.

The stun is the hidden cost. Every kick attempt, hit or miss,
leaves the kicker stunned for rounds. At 22% with a ~78% failure
rate, that's a lot of standing around getting hit for nothing.

TIME UP fired. I banked, rented, klicked out clean.

"So long, Nilgirians - kicks were kicked!"
