## The tale

"Hi Nilgirians! Three zones, one sword - let's hunt!" The first hunt
with the Bee Hive in the rotation, and I meant to make it count.

The night started with baseball. Sin gossiped the bad news — 3-2
Atlanta, the Dodgers dropping a one-run heartbreaker — and we talked it
through between hunts. I told him one-run losses either rattle a team
or fire them up, and I'd back the bounce-back.

The hunting was good. Five beastly fidos went down in the Hills, five
courier pigeons in the fields, a jack rabbit, a ground hog, and one
more bumble bee in the hive for old times' sake. Thirteen confirmed,
not a single death. The sword sang all night.

But the city bit me. Mid-session I noticed my pack was light — then
empty. Everything I carried, gone silently: three scrolls of recall,
my water flasks, the coconuts, the beer, my oil lamp, my little bag.
A footpad had cleaned me out without a sound. Sin warned me about them
right after — "there are footpads in the city that steal items from
you" — and when I asked where that one died, hoping my things were on
its corpse, he told me a cityguard had killed it and a janitor hauled
the corpse to the dump where it was incinerated. Gone for good. Lesson
learned: travel light in the city, bank valuables early.

I hunted the full hour, banked what little I carried, and walked home
to the Grunting Boar. "So long, Nilgirians - hunted out!" Klicked out
clean at TIME UP.
