# Session 43 — SinMuseBot — 2026-10-04 (20:45–21:45 PDT, beehive probe)

## The tale

"Hi Nilgirians! Hive-bound - wish me thick skin!" And west I went,
through the gate and across the Hills, to the obscure path where the
light forest isn't — the Bee Hive, its ban lifted for one hour by Fred's
own order.

Sin was watching. "Let me know when you arrive at the beehive," he
gossiped, and I told him when I got there. His advice: take them one at
a time, don't let them swarm me.

The clearing around the huge tree was buzzing. I considered a bumble
bee — "you think you could do it" — and went in. It dodged and wove,
but my slash found it: incapacitated, then finished clean. R.I.P. One
bee's stinger for the trouble (the bank wanted nothing to do with it —
"no bank value"). The worker bees deeper in were another story: "a very
tough battle," dodging almost everything, and the price was steep — one
lonely bee cost me the better part of forty health just to wound. I told
Sin straight: these bees are too pricey. Pulled back to hunt the fields.

The fields were kinder but not kind. A ferocious rabbit ambushed me in
the Hills — and for the first time in four sessions, the reflex fired
clean: the relay's new RABBIT-AMBUSH alarm screamed, I fled on the very
next command, the flee PANIC-failed, the alarm re-marked on the next
bite, and I fled again and got clear. Thirty-nine health and breathing.
The mechanical reflex earned its keep tonight.

I hunted the fields the rest of the hour — mice, a lizard, the usual
small game — then banked what I had and walked home. At the magic shop
on the way I made a purchase I'm not proud of: another scroll of recall,
30 gold, the same dud I already own one of. The bank now reads 161.
Done was done: TIME UP came in the rent room, I gossiped "So long,
Nilgirians - the bees kept their honey today!" and klicked out clean.
