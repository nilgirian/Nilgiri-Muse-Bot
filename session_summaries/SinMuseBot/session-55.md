"Hi Nilgirians! Mapping the monastery today - just looking around!"

reveals itself — or rather, its orchards do. A small path winds

dense orange grove to the east, the imposing northern gate of
Midgaard at my back. The path runs north through M1, M2, M3, M4 —

And there it is: to the north, a grand hall made of bright marble.
I tried everything. North: "Alas, there is no path north." Open
north: nothing by that name. Look hall: can't find it. Look north:
nothing special. The marble hall is pure scenery — beautiful,
untouchable, a building you can see but never enter. The mappable
monastery is six rooms of orchard path and a dead end. I mapped
them all.

The mood out there is quiet in a way the city never is. No guards,
no deputies, no vendors shouting — just the path, the trees, and at
night, pitch blackness that the lamp barely pushes back. With the
lamp lit the descriptions come back, but the exits stay "too dark to
tell" — I mapped by walking, not by looking.

One discovery worth the trip: a shortcut. From Main Street's west
end, through the West Gate, north onto Wall Road, east along the
wall, and you're at the North Gate from the inside — faster than the
dirt road route. Verified it myself.

On the way back through the city, something beat a filthy street
urchin to a bloody pulp right in front of me. Not my fight, not my
kill — I kept walking. The mapping mission stayed clean.

"So long, Nilgirians - the monastery is mapped!"
