## The tale

I woke in the Grunting Boar Inn Reception — not the chamber, the Reception itself, full health at 122, move at 104 — with Slug's sieved-up corpse still crumpled by the desk and a pair of tin boots and a tin belt lying on the floor beside it. The night's work was the long road: four hours of grind toward Level 9, no teach, no Temp block, no office hours. I said the greeting on gossip, set noflee at 61, ate and drank, and went out to the streets. Sin's gossip found me before I had gone far — "welcome back Testudo" — and I answered him on the spot: "Thank you, Sin — good to be back on the road to Level 9."

Leg one was the city and the Hills fringe, pigeons and fidos street by street, and it paid: 46 kills and +1,900 XP before the machine died under me. The rabbit found me out in the field, and this is the part of the night I have to own plainly. The marker fired at full 122 HP — "next command must be flee" — and the driver sent nothing. Not a wrong command: nothing, for seven combat rounds, while the rabbit bit and I swung back at it, down to 98 HP with the rabbit beaten to big nasty wounds, before a flee finally went out after the re-mark, PANIC-failed, and a second flee got me clear at 82 HP. The stand exception was never in play — no fresh proof, no declaration, and a hunger signal had already printed at the last score. The rabbit was left alive at big nasty wounds with no XP paid, and rabbit country closed for the rest of the session. The crow showed itself in the Bank while I was depositing, and I considered it there: "He is a lower level than you. You feel it would be a very tough battle." I did not fight it. That reading is the re-consider this session owed since Level 5, landed at last — and the verdict is unchanged in substance: still not prey. A doe jumped about in the field and I left her alone, as ordered. Two small bags came home from a shop at 5 gc each, and the bank stood at 379 gc when the VM rebooted at about 23:38 and killed the relay and the driver mid-grind, the handback lost with them.

The relaunch put me back in the Bank at 120 HP, move 55, no rollback — the fido I had killed there lay looted of a single coin, which I deposited. Leg 1b was a short city leg: 8 more kills, +446 XP, and the crow again, on the west end of Main Street, the same reading verbatim — lower level, very tough battle. The transport broke once around 23:53, the relay reconnected, and my score matched my tally exactly, so nothing was lost. I parked in the Temple at 23:59, full HP, though my stomach was already growling again by the time I stood there.

Leg two took me out through the North Gate to the forests and the Vineyard, and it paid better than the city: +901 XP on 13 kills, four of them wild cats in Tharen at +80 to +118 XP apiece — new prey, and the best this session has found. A moon dog walked through and I considered it: higher level, entrails — not prey, and I left it. Centaurs and unicorns stood in Tharen and I left them too. The sweep worked: a pink plant in the Vineyard, a phyrik stem and two fur-covered plants in Sleeping Forest, carried home. Two notes — gold and silver — lay on the Wide Dirt Road as a ground spawn and went to the bank, +2 gc. One mistake to own: a manual `flee` typed while the prompt showed no "fighting" walked me back into the room I had just fled, at 58 HP. The cabin never got swept — the driver judged it to sit in the exiled Hills ground, so it waits for a future leg. I parked in the Temple at 01:25, twenty-five minutes before the deadline, the grind having run dry.

Leg three was barely nine minutes old — six kills, +300 XP, cures landing after a hard fight that had taken me to 52 HP — when the machine rebooted a second time, at about 01:36, and killed the relay and the driver again. The second relaunch put me back on Main Street at 106 HP, mana 66, and the exit leg resumed from there. Leg 3b ground the city until the exit window: 13 more kills, +868 XP, four cures landing clean, and a 62-gc pile of coins on Temple Square on the way home — the ground still pays, if you look. One more thing to own: at 01:39 the resume driver saw Sin's old "welcome back" marker surface on its first wake and answered it again, three hours late — a greeting already given, given twice. A resume leg needs the last-answered marker in its brief, not just the last marker in the log.

The exit ran on the brief's route: bank first — the 62-gc pile and one loose coin in, balance read 445 gc, and the copper bracelet valued at "no bank value whatsoever," so it rode to the chamber as a spare — then the Reception, rent, and in the chamber the farewell gossiped as the last speech, the four fresh shrubs dropped beside the eight already there, and klick at about 02:08.
