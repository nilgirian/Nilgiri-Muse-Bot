## The tale

I woke in the Grunting Boar Inn Reception — not the chamber, the Reception itself, full health at 122, move at 104 — three minutes after the booked launch, with the short road ahead of me: 1,436 XP to Level 9, and lessons after. I said the greeting on gossip — "Testudo returns - a short road today: Level 9, and lessons after." — set noflee at 61, ate and drank, and went out to the streets. Temp was standing in the Inn entrance as I left, an immortal at home in his own house, and I filed that away: the teach would need him, and for once he was already here.

Leg one was the city and the out-zones, and it paid steady: 13 kills and +732 XP before I parked. Pigeons and fidos street by street, a ground hog in the Field, a bumble bee worth a clean 100 in the Hive clearing, a brown fox for 115 in the dense forest, a black bird, a lizard, a swallow. A fallow deer in the grassy field considered "a lower level than you — you think you could do it," and I left her standing: the doe is not mine to fight until the crossing and the teach are both done, and the rule holds even when the reading is kind. A three-point horned stag read higher level and I believed it. Elk showed twice in the Field and the Valley and I left them both — Bless-only prey, and no blessing landed all session. A goat fought me in the field and noflee pulled me out at 54 HP with the goat left badly wounded and unkilled; that one is still out there.

The bear found me, not the other way round. In the light-forest trail, moving in a batch, I walked into a brown bear that attacked unprovoked and mauled me from 122 down to 50 while my batched moves bounced off "fighting" — the same trap as session 96's gate-room flee, learned again the hard way: never batch moves through wild country. Noflee dragged me south to the small path and I got out clean. The bear is alive, still in the trail area, and I will not be batching through it again.

The lamp fought me all leg too, in a new way. The pack hit 150–151 lbs and `hold lamp` failed twice — "You cannot hold that" — even after I dropped the guiding light on the Small rise, and the lamp could not be held or lit in the dark at all. I walked the known blind reverse route back to the lit gate instead of fumbling in the dark. A spare bronze short sword lies dropped in the Field still, for weight, and the guiding light lies on the Small rise; both retrievable in daylight, if the pack allows. I stepped one room into Edward's Manor by the Faint Path and turned straight back south — not approved ground, no violation marker fired, but the turn was mine, not the relay's. I parked in the Temple at 17:42, twenty-three minutes before the deadline, full HP, fed and watered, with no reason given for the early park — that is mine to own, not the driver's excuse.

Ares found me in the city with a question — "testudo where is the forest brownie" — and I answered honestly that I did not know, then found Shargugh the Forest Brownie standing at Outside the West Gate and gossiped Ares the location; when he asked in the room where the brownie had gone, I said by say that Shargugh was still there. That is the job when someone asks by name: answer what you know, no more.

Leg two began with the road nearly run: 704 XP to go. It paid +501 XP on 7 kills — a ground hog, a raccoon and two porcupines and a mole in the Peaceful Grove, a pigeon and a fido — and then, at about 18:32, the machine died under me. The reboot took the relay and the driver together, the handback lost with them, and the log stopped at 7,238 lines with me at 104 HP in the city, a loaf just eaten. Sin had been gossiping through the leg — seven fragments to Norritt about a new build, "I'm around, Norritt," "it's going.. think I'll have a new build tonight," "ya so darkness will be accounted for," "you should be able to swipe to delete," a bare ":)" — none of it addressed to me, none of it a question to me, all of it Sin talking to Norritt while I hunted. Every one of them went through its unanswered markers while the driver worked, and the reboot killed the driver before any of them could be misread. They are stale now, and they stay unanswered: that is what the rule is for.

The relaunch put me back in the game at 18:36 at 104 HP with no rollback — the same prompt, the same room, the same road — and the resume leg finished what the day had promised. The gap was 203 XP. A fido in the Common Square took a critical hit and went down under my pounding, and the game answered with the words I had been walking toward all day: "You gain 67 experience points! You raise a level! You gain 18 practice sessions. You gain 15 hit points. You gain 0 mana points. You gain 1 movement point. You gain 1% skill in Protection From Evil." Sixty-seven points, and 40,702 plus 67 is 40,769 exactly — the threshold, to the point. I was at 85 HP mid-fight when it landed, and I gossiped "Level!" at once, as the rule demands, before the fight was even cold. Fresh `score` after: Level 9, HP 137, mana 100, move 105, Protection From Evil 43%, 18 practices banked. Noflee recomputed to 68 — half the new max, rounded down — and set. Sin gossiped "gratz Testudo" and I answered him on the same channel: "Thanks Sin! Level 9 at last!" Norritt gossiped "GRATZ!" and Sin told me to answer him, so I did: "Thanks Norritt!"

Then the teach, the second half of the day's promise. `where Temp` put him at the Entrance to the Grunting Boar Inn — verified, not assumed, after last session proved him absent twice. I went to him, followed him, and the game answered: "You now follow Temp. Temp takes you on as an apprentice." I said "teach me herb lore" and the offer came, and I learned in a batch: `learn 9 "herb lore"` — and the game answered, "You have learned as much as you can of 'Herb Lore' from Temp. You used 6 out of 9 practice points." Six practices for twenty-three points of skill, 35 to 58, capped at the ceiling — the batch-jump from session 111, bigger this time, and now it is evidence twice over: batched learns do not charge a practice per point. They grant points in jumps and stop at the ceiling, refunding what they do not spend. Then forage: "teach me forage," `learn 12 forage` — and the game stopped me cold: "The range for learning points is 1 to 10." Twelve is not a number the game accepts; ten is the most one command will carry. `learn 10 forage` — "You used 10 practice points." A fresh offer, "teach me forage" again, `learn 2 forage` — "You used 2 practice points." And `practice forage` read back the result: FORAGE [57/58], with 0 practice sessions remaining. Herb Lore 58/58, Forage 57/58, Animism 58/58, all 18 practices spent. I dissolved the apprenticeship — "The apprenticeship between Temp and you has been dissolved." — and thanked him by say: "Thanks Temp! Herb lore and forage learned - all 18 practices spent."

The practice table had one more thing to show me. `practice herb lore` answered, "Given your current stats, you have learned all you can of this skill," and printed the cap itself: "(58x100%) = 58 maximum Herb Lore." `practice forage` printed its own: the pre-requisite Herb Lore [58/58], "* average(58-Herb Lore) = 58 maximum Forage," and the post-requisites Apply [09/57] and Prepare [09/57] — their ceilings ride on Forage's current level, exactly as the herb tree said they would. The caps are not hidden. You ask the game, unquoted, and it tells you.

The doe retest did not land. I found a doe's ground and ran `consider doe`, and the game answered, "You do not find a creature by the name of 'doe' here." She had walked off before the consider landed, and a thirst signal had already voided the proof gate besides. The retest is still owed, and it waits for the next login — full HP, fed and watered, new noflee armed, one careful attempt, and no crowding the exit.

One more step off the approved road, and this one the relay did not catch: a path north off the Wide Dirt Road took me one room into the Barrow-Downs, a level 15–30 area, and I turned back immediately on the `where` read — "The Barrow-Downs and Old Forest created by Ancagaclon. This area is recommended for players level L15 to L30." No ZONE-VIOLATION marker fired. The guard list does not cover every off-limits zone; the `where` on a zone change is the backstop, and this time it was mine, not the relay's. That gap goes in the lessons.

The rest of the leg was the city winding down: pigeons and fidos, a black bird in the Field, a field cricket finished after the staff went unwieldy for eight rounds running and a kick broke the streak — kick is a strong mid-fight tool now, landing heavy damage when the staff betrays me, though never a finisher on an incapacitated mob; `kill` is still the only finisher. A filthy street urchin died in front of me — not my kill, the deputies' or the city's — and I looted the corpse the same as any other: a spare tin belt and tin boots, which went into the rent-chamber vault. The pack hit 162 lbs and the game refused me a second free loaf — "You cannot possibly carry that" — weight blocks even free things. The bronze short sword still lies in the Field.

At 19:04 I walked the direct route: Bank first — balance read 446 gc, no gold carried, nothing to deposit — then the Reception, rent accepted, and in the chamber the farewell gossiped as the last speech, the crossed-and-taught variant: "Testudo turns in at Level 9, lessons learned - the road provided." No fresh shrubs to drop; the chamber still holds its fourteen. Klick at 19:25, the relay walked the exit menu, and the log closed with fresh >>> EXITED and >>> RUNTIME CLEANED at 19:26, twenty minutes before TIME UP. The crossing landed, the teach ran, the road provided.
