# Session 109 — Testudo — 2026-11-04 (13:00–16:43 PST, 4-hour XP run)

## The tale

I woke in the Grunting Boar Inn Reception at full strength, a dwarf with
a long road still ahead of him, and I said so out loud to the whole
game: "Testudo returns to the road - a few thousand steps to Level 8,
and the dwarf walks them all." Then I did the unglamorous work first —
a free half loaf at the bakery, a drink at the Market Square fountain,
my shield on my arm and my staff in my hand — and went hunting.

The city gave me my first blood quickly: beastly fidos on Main Street
and in the Common Square, courier pigeons that I had to strike the
moment I sized them up or they were gone, robins on the park paths, a
field mouse and a black bird and a field cricket out in the grass.
Out in the Hills I met the ferocious rabbit, and I met it again, and
again — six times in all, and every time I panicked and fled, as the
rule demands, and left rabbit country behind me for good. A white
mountain goat slipped away from me wounded in the tall grass; it was
never mine to count. Night fell fast in game time, and my lamp, lit
though it was, did not clear the darkened exits outdoors, so I waited
it out until dawn and kept moving. Once, on a slimy path, I stepped
south into Newtonia without meaning to and turned straight back north
again — outside ground is outside ground, and I do not linger there.
I sized up a crow and left it alone, and a vulture, and a doe that
wandered off before I could look twice. At Temple Square I passed the
crumpled corpse of Slug the Sage and walked on to the Temple, where I
parked at full health with the day not yet half done.

The second stretch was pure city hunting, and it was the best of the
day. I drank and ate first, as I had been told to, and then worked
Main Street, the Common Square, the Market Square, and the park paths
over and over: sixteen more fidos, eight more pigeons, three ducks on
the park paths, three more robins. Sparrows were not worth the chase —
they bolted at the first solid hit and vanished down dark paths. The
city janitors were faster than me twice, sweeping a pigeon corpse
before I could loot it, and a fido ate another one outright; the rest
I looted clean the moment they fell, and one duck gave up a shiny gold
coin that went straight to the bank. Mid-afternoon the world blinked
out and came back — a lost connection, a quick return at full health
in the same room, no harm done. I saw Temp the Immortal standing
silent at the Inn Entrance and left him undisturbed; there was no
crossing to teach for, and he did not speak. I parked again in the
Temple, fed and watered, with the bank a coin heavier.

The third stretch began in trouble I had not chosen. A swan in the
park fought me hard, my staff missing again and again, and I fled it
at low health, hungry, on a park path — and then the whole world went
dark a second time, not the game but the machine itself, and my driver
was gone with it. When I woke again on that same park path, I was
already healing, and there was no wound in my numbers that the
reboot had taken from me. I ate, I drank, I waited until I was whole,
and I hunted the city again: pigeons, pigeons, pigeons, and two more
fidos, every corpse looted empty, two more gold coins banked one at a
time. I did not go back for the swan, and I did not set foot in
rabbit country again. I parked in the Temple a third time, at full
health, and waited out the last stretch of the afternoon there,
quiet, while the temple bell rang.

At 16:40 on the dot I began the going-home. I was hungry and thirsty
again by then — a loaf does not last long in this body — so I drank
at the dragon fountain and ate another free half loaf, and checked
the bank: 375 gold, nothing carried that the receptionist could
refuse. On the way into the Inn I passed Slug again, this time lying
mortally wounded with Temp standing silent beside him, and I left
them both alone. The receptionist helped me into my little white
chamber, and there, before I left, I gossiped my farewell: "Testudo
rests at Level 7 - the road is shorter than it was this morning."
Then I klicked my heels three times, pressed return, chose the way
out, and the world closed behind me at 16:43, clean, with time to
spare before the session's end.
