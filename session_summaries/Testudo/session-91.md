# Session 91 — The Rolling of Testudo

*2026-10-24, 10:46–11:56 PDT. The creation of the first leveling-guide test character. Testudo, Level 1.*

## The tale

I was rolled 573 times before I was allowed to be someone.

That is the honest arithmetic of my birth. The dice that make a dwarf in this world are four-sided handfuls — four dice, the lowest thrown away — and they do not conspire: a strong arm does not steal from a wise head, each stat stands or falls alone, and the combination my makers wanted, a constitution like a fortress wall with strength and wisdom standing beside it, comes up perhaps six times in a thousand. So they rolled, and rolled, and I flickered through lives. Once — roll 129, the chronicle remembers — I stood almost perfect: constitution at the very ceiling of what dice can make, wisdom high, strength high. But the watchers that day were waiting for a star to appear beside my numbers, a mark the Grok Bot had described to them, and on this world's screens no such star exists. They let that life go, waiting for a sign that was never coming. There is a lesson in my bones before I have even swung a weapon: verify even a teacher's words at the screen.

The run that finally kept me very nearly didn't happen either. My first making snagged on a vanity — dwarven hair, it turns out, may only be medium, long, or very long, and the script insisted on short, and argued with the world about it forever. The second making starved on the star that wasn't there. The third ran with its eyes open: totals, not omens. Constitution eighteen. Wisdom fifteen. Strength seventeen. A mind quick enough, a face pleasant enough — and a dexterity of five, the price the dice exacted, for I am not and never will be nimble. I am a tortoise made flesh: slow, and very hard to kill. The name, chosen before any of this was known, turned out to be prophecy.

Then a password came by letter to a mailbox in another world, and my maker spoke it, and I was saved — that is the moment a character becomes real here, the rest is ceremony. The ceremony, admittedly, fumbled: the script dropped me at the threshold menu like a parcel and vanished. So my first steps were guided by hand instead, and I count that a better omen anyway. I woke in the Temple of Midgaard, full and quenched, three manna in my pack and a copy of the Nilgiri Guide — homework, from birth. I spoke my first words to the whole world at once: a dwarf named Testudo, newly rolled, bound for the long road to level ten. I read my own measure — thirty-six hits in this small frame, a hundred mana, eighty-five move — and walked south past a sheriff's deputy and a courier pigeon, east into the Grunting Boar, up the stairs, and rented a room I could not have paid for and was not charged for. There I spoke my farewell and `klick`ed out of the world, one hour old, zero experience, entirely ready.
