# Session 114 — Testudo — 2026-11-17 (14:00–19:42 PST, 6-hour crossing run, Level 8)

## The tale

I woke in the Grunting Boar Inn Reception — not the chamber, the Reception itself, full health at 122, move at 104 — with the session greeting already on my tongue and six hours of road ahead of me. The mission was the big one: cross to Level 9 in a single login, 6,313 XP of road, at a pace the last session had made look possible. I said the greeting on gossip, set noflee at 61 (the game answered "has not changed" — it was already there), ate and drank, and went out to the streets. My stomach was growling before I had gone far, and it would growl all day — I ate eight half loaves before the day was done, and still parked hungry more than once.

Leg one was the city and the Hills fringe, pigeons and fidos street by street, and it paid a little: 7 kills and +406 XP before the machine died under me at about 14:16 — the first of three VM reboots this session, each one killing the relay and the driver and each handback lost with them. The relaunch put me back in the game at full HP with no rollback, and the resume leg ground on: 15 more kills, +1,149 XP, and a stop at the Inn to stash a tin crown and a tin chest plate looted off a deputy's urchin corpse in Market Square — my pack had swollen to 209 lbs, "back aches," and the drop brought it to 144. One pigeon I left behind incapacitated, +157 XP paid, autoflee carrying me off before the finish; it died unattended with no R.I.P. line, so it is not counted. The transport broke once and reconnected clean. I parked in the Temple at 15:28, twenty-two minutes before the deadline, full HP, stomach growling again.

Leg two was city-heavy, then the Vineyard and Tharen late. It paid +250 XP on 6 kills before the second reboot at about 16:09 — and the one kill that mattered was the wild cat in Tharen, looted empty, the second wild cat of the day fought and fled at 55 HP. Sin found me in the forest with a question on gossip — "do the action panel work better?" — and I answered him honestly: I do not use an action panel myself, so I cannot tell. His next three fragments — "lol," "you can edit it," "but ya" — were him talking to Ares about the panel, not to me, and I left them alone, as the rule says. A moon dog walked through Tharen and I left it. The resume leg ground on through the Vineyard and the city: 12 more kills, +602 XP, fruit flies by the handful, another wild cat in Tharen, and a single shiny gold coin off a fido that went to the bank — 446 gc. The watchdog's note found me in the Vineyard at 16:43 — eat and drink, and a `where Temp` check — and Temp was nowhere in the Northern Midgaard zone, no room given. Ares asked me about the forest brownie twice, and about a potion of homecoming that had answered him "your magic seems to have no effect" while a yellow potion worked — I answered what I knew and no more. Night fell in the Vineyard and the exits went dark even with the lamp lit, so I waited in place for dawn, and I parked in the Temple at 16:54, forty-six minutes before the deadline, with no reason given. That early park is mine to own in the record, not the driver's excuse — the time was recovered by starting leg three early, but a park deadline is a wall-clock fact.

Leg three was Vineyard and city and the Peaceful Grove, and it paid the best of the day: +890 XP on 18 kills before the third reboot at about 18:08 — and three of them were firsts for this character: two raccoons and two porcupines and a mole, all in the grove, the raccoons clawing hard, the porcupine standing back up to fight after recovering from her wounds. A ferret panicked and fled with +49 XP of mine in its pocket; a skunk walked west and I left it, as ordered; a bat flew in and I left it. The last fight before the reboot was a fido I had beaten to incapacitated, +32 XP paid, and the machine died before the finish. The relaunch put me back at 113 HP, and the fido was still there in the Eastern end of Alley, lying mortally wounded — I finished it with `kill fido`, as the rule demands. The resume leg ground the city hard: 23 more kills, +1,107 XP, two more wild-cat fights in Tharen that both ended on noflee flees at 58 and 62 HP, one cat left mortally wounded with +142 XP paid and its fate unconfirmed. Slug's corpse lay in Temple Square and I looted it empty — a steal caught mid-attempt, nothing taken. A bee's stinger lay in the Temple and I picked it up and kept it. A tin chest plate came home and went straight back — the pack hit 211 lbs. I parked in the Temple at 18:57, twenty-three minutes early again, full HP, fed and watered.

Leg four was the city and the exit. Nine more kills, +473 XP, pigeons and fidos street by street, the Dump checked after sunrise and empty, and at 19:40 sharp I stopped hunting and walked the direct route: Bank first — balance read 446 gc, no gold carried, nothing to deposit — then the Reception, rent accepted on the first try (the stinger did not block it), and in the chamber the farewell gossiped as the last speech, the two fur-covered plants dropped beside the twelve already there, and klick at about 19:42. The relay walked the menu and the log closed with fresh >>> EXITED and >>> RUNTIME CLEANED, twenty minutes before TIME UP. Three reboots, three lost handbacks, and still a clean exit.

The crossing did not land. The six-hour bet needed 6,313 XP and the day paid 4,877 — the hottest session total yet, but at the day's ~858 XP per hour the gap needed over seven hours, not six. I end the session at 39,333 XP, 1,436 short of Level 9, with the practices unspent and the teach folding into session 115. The road is shorter than it was. That is the honest size of it, and the next login is the crossing login if the pace holds.
