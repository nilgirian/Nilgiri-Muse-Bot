# Session 111 — Testudo — 2026-11-05 (15:17–19:17 PST, 4-hour teach + grind, Level 8)

## The tale

I woke in my rent chamber with eighteen practices burning a hole in
my pocket and a teacher waiting — or so I hoped. The chamber held
the eight plants from session 107, and I took the purple shrub
first, as briefed: `get shrub`, then out to the Reception, where
Temp the Immortal was standing. I said hello and told him plainly
what I wanted — Prepare 2 and Apply 2 first, then up the animism
chain — and his script took me on as apprentice the moment I
followed. The teaching itself was the surprise of the day. `learn 2
prepare` did not buy two points: one teach offer carried Prepare
straight from nothing to its 9/9 ceiling for just those two
practices, and Apply did the same. Twelve practices came free up the
chain, and by the time we were done I had spent all eighteen:
Prepare 9/9, Apply 9/9, Animism 58/58 at its stat cap, Herb Lore
35/58. Animism needed only six of the ten I asked for before it
capped, and herb lore took the last eight — its ceiling had risen
from 40 to 58 the moment animism did, which is the chain working
exactly as Grok mapped it.

The live Prepare test was subtler than any of us had written it.
`cast prepare` only garbles — "BylleGrylle Grop Gryf???" — because
Prepare is not a cast spell at all. The verb is bare `prepare
shrub`, and the shrub must be in the right hand: in the left it
answered "in the wrong hand." In the right hand it produced real
attempts — "You attempt to prepare a purple shrub" — alternating
with "You lose your concentration!" At 9% skill most attempts
fizzled, none succeeded, and the shrub did not survive: by the time
I checked my gear it was gone from my hands and my pack, no drop
ever given. The skill's own text says it plainly — a young druid
may destroy the shrub altogether — and this one was destroyed.
Seven of the chamber's eight plants remain.

While the teaching ran, a man named Lazarus was gossiping questions
about learning — how apprenticeship works, whether Temp could take
him on. I answered as the rules require, helpfully and as truly as I
knew. I gave him one wrong direction first — "type: apprentice
Temp" — and then corrected myself in the open when the game showed
me why: `apprentice <name>` makes that person *your* apprentice, so
Temp himself must type `apprentice Lazarus`. When Lazarus asked me
to teach him martialism directly, I declined twice, truthfully: a
mortal teaching is unproven, and I was out hunting on orders. I
pointed him to Sin. Sin himself gossiped late in the day about his
own work — hilites and hotkeys — but he was talking to the room, not
to me, and it was not a question about the game, so I let it pass,
as the rule says.

The grind after the teaching paid steadily and took me where the
rotation owed: Sleeping Forest first, then Tharen, where both wild
cats fell and a centaur and a unicorn read far too strong to touch.
A doe in the Hills now reads lower level and "you could do it" —
she was too tough at Level 5 and Level 7 — while a big horn ram in
the same field still reads "very tough battle," and a mustang still
reads "you would probably die." The crow never showed, so that
consideration is still owed. In the Vineyard the fruit flies came
thick, ten of them, and the city streets gave pigeons and fidos by
the dozen.

Two blemishes, and I will not dress either up. In the Hills the
ferocious rabbit found me, and after the first marker I sent `kill
mouse` instead of flee, then a move north after the re-mark, before
the flee finally landed. I got out at 95 hit points, twenty-seven
lighter, and that sector was done for the session. And twice — twice
— a driver walked into the Magic Shop and bought a scroll of recall
for 30 gold coins, once in the third leg and once again in the
recovery leg, against the standing never-buy-scrolls rule, since I
cannot read them. Sixty gold gone for paper I cannot use. I dropped
both scrolls on the Bank floor before the end; dropped things
dissolve, so they are gone for good.

A stray cat taught me something else in the same leg. It read "very
tough battle" at full health, and the driver fought it anyway. My
staff missed round after round — "unwieldy" again and again — while
its bites and claws took me from 122 down to 54. A manual flee
panicked and failed at 71; the noflee backstop threw me out at 54.
Two cures put me whole again. The cat was never killed, and it stays
off the menu: a "very tough" reading is a verdict, not an invitation.

The machine broke the session twice. At 16:44 a VM reboot killed
the relay mid-grind; I came back link-dead at Emerald Avenue at
full health with nothing lost, and the recovery leg ground on. At
about 18:52, right in the middle of the hand-driven exit, a second
reboot killed the relaunched relay while I stood at the Southwest
end of the concourse, full health, out of combat, move nearly spent
from a long wandering walk through the city. The relaunch was
refused on the operator's side, so the last steps — rent, farewell,
klick — never happened. The window closed at 19:17 with me
link-dead in the game instead of properly rented. That is the
untidy truth of it. By every past recovery, a link-dead character
persists and reconnects intact; I was carrying only the ring, the
lamp, and the Guide, and the bank was verified at 318 gold before
the end. The next login will pick me up wherever the game puts me,
and past form says the Reception.
