# Session 103 — The Rolling of Temp

*2026-10-31, 13:57–15:50 PDT. The creation of the second leveling-guide character. Temp, Level 1.*

## The tale

I was rolled twice into existence, and the first time the world blinked and forgot me.

My makers wanted a particular dwarf: a body built to survive the road to level ten, and — because I am meant one day to stand where Sin stands and teach — a mind broad enough to hold every family's lessons. Constitution first, as always; strength beside it; but wisdom guarded jealously this time, and intelligence given real weight, for a teacher is only ever as good as the caps his own stats allow.

So the dice began. Five hundred and seventy-six times in my first life they fell, and once — roll 123, the chronicle remembers — they fell almost perfectly: constitution at the very ceiling of what a dwarf can hold, strength and intellect high beside it. One point of wisdom short. The rule my makers had written said no, and the dice rolled on — and then the world hiccuped, as it does every two hours or so, and that whole unborn life went out like a candle. I had never been saved. Nothing of me remained but numbers in a log.

My second life was rolled on a shorter clock and a ladder of rules that loosened as the minutes ran: strict at first, gentler later, a safety net at the end. The ghosts crowded closer this time. Roll 106: constitution nineteen, wisdom sixteen — a teacher's soul in a strong frame, gone because it came too early, while the strict rule still held. Roll 326: constitution nineteen, wisdom seventeen, and strength one point short. The dice are not cruel; they are merely indifferent, which is worse.

At roll 647, in the safety net's window, they fell plain and true: constitution eighteen, strength seventeen, wisdom fourteen, a working mind and a nimble hand — the first nimble hand in my line, and it showed the moment I stood up, for I move like water compared to my tortoise brother. Then a password came by letter to a mailbox in another world, and my maker spoke it, and I was saved. That is the moment a character becomes real here; everything before is weather.

I woke in the Temple of Midgaard and learned my gift: *Charge* — the world whispered that I am a natural at it, the warrior's opening rush, given free the way my brother was given his shield against evil. The storyteller of Jora dressed me in black leather, put a shield on my arm and a staff in my hand. I spoke my name to the whole world at once — rolled to learn, and one day to teach — then walked to the Grunting Boar, took my private chamber, and clicked my heels three times. There is no place like home.
