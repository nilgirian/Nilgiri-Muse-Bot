# The Bee Hive

**Recommended level L1** (per `where`: "The Bee Hive created by Mobius.
This area is recommended for players level L1"). **Status: OFF-LIMITS**
(Fred's ban, 2026-09-28 — do not enter; lifted for sessions 35, 43, 44
only by explicit override).

**How to get there:** Hills and Plains → Hills (the room north of
Field) → West: "An obscure path into the light forest". Despite the
light-forest wording, the room's zone is The Bee Hive — room appearance
is not zone identity; always `where` in a new room. (Session 44 found a
second Hills-adjacent room, "An obscure path OUT OF the light forest,"
with East: Hills — likely a separate room, not yet confirmed distinct.)

## Rooms

### Entry and clearing (sessions 35, 43)
- **An obscure path into the light forest** — entry room; `where` reads
  The Bee Hive zone; exits East (Hills) and West (deeper).
- **A clearing around a huge tree** — one room west of entry. Partial
  clearing around a large tree to the southwest; exits East (entry),
  West ("A small path in the dense forest"), South ("A way up the huge
  tree" — the climb starts here). Contents seen: worker bees, bumble
  bees, a black crow, a small fern. Session 43: bumble bee R.I.P.'d here.

### The tree climb (session 44) — leads to the hive entrance
- **A way up the huge tree** — low branches for climbing; buzzing
  louder toward the hive. Exits: South (clearing), Up ("On the trunk of
  the huge tree").
- **On the trunk of the huge tree** — medium branches. Exits: Up
  ("Start of the branch"), Down ("A way down the huge tree").
- **A way down the huge tree** — alternate descent. Exits: South
  (clearing), Up ("On the trunk of the huge tree").
- **Start of the branch** — the large branch supporting the hive juts
  west over the clearing; can't climb higher. Exits: West ("On the
  branch"), Down ("On the trunk of the huge tree").
- **On the branch** — halfway out; "a fall from this height might prove
  to be fatal." Exits: East ("Start of the branch"), West ("Thickest
  part of the branch").
- **Thickest part of the branch** — "A very large bee hive hangs down
  below from the branch." Exits: East ("On the branch"), **Down
  ("Entrance to the hive") — NOT ENTERED** (queen/drone/keeper
  territory; Fred's standing avoid order). This is the end of safe
  mapping.

### Forest paths (session 44)
- **A small path in the dense forest** — west of the clearing; forest
  lightens to the west. Exits: North (another "small path in the dense
  forest"), East (clearing), West ("An intersection in the light forest").
- **An intersection in the light forest** — paths north to "a small
  field," east/west into dense forest. Exits: North ("On a small, grassy
  field" — not visited), East/West ("A small path in the dense forest").
- **An intersection in the dense forest** — twilight under old trees.
  Exits: North/East/South (all dense-forest paths, partially mapped).
- **A small path through the light forest** — tall slender trees; paths
  north/south. Contents: a funny looking old gnome (smiling, not
  engaged).
- **A trail through the light forest** — east to the forest edge, west
  deeper. Exits: East/West ("too dark to tell" at night).
- **An obscure path out of the light forest** — break between gray trees;
  "the path dumps out east into the fields." Exits: East (Hills), West
  (clearing). Possibly a second Hills entrance; distinct from the "into"
  room pending confirmation.
