Zone: **Midgaard Monastery**, created by Mobius, recommended
**L2 to L20** (`where` verified at the Archway in the Cloister).
This is the INDOOR monastery — temple, cloister ring, two monastery
buildings, and the temple garden — distinct from the outdoor orchard
path mapped in `midgaard-monastery.md` (session 55). The two are
believed to connect through the Archway's locked north portcullis
(the "northern wall road outside the north gate"), not yet passed.

**How to get there:** Temple of Midgaard (login point) → `open
portcullis` if shut → NORTH → The North End of the Temple of
Midgaard. The temple portcullis **auto-closes quietly after a delay**
("The portcullis north quietly shuts closed.") — re-open it on the
way back; it is not locked from either side (contrast the Archway
portcullis below).

**Character of the place:** entirely peaceful. No mob showed any
aggression across two full passes, day and night. The population is
temple clergy (humanoid NPCs) plus one giant snail. As a hunting
survey the verdict is negative: the only favorable consider readings
are acolytes and student acolytes — humanoid NPCs, not lawful prey
under our rules — and everything else reads "probably die" or worse
at L6. Come here to explore, not to hunt.

## East monastery building (via the door in Cloister east seg.2)

- **Vaulted Hall, western end** — wide white-marble hall, vaulted
  ceiling, doors N and S. Exits: N -> Winery; E -> Vaulted Hall
  (eastern end); W -> Cloister (door); S -> Sitting Room.
- **Winery** — red-stained walls/floor, grape smell, a gutter along
  the west wall; a large vat and a large crate. Exits: S -> Vaulted
  Hall (west). An adept stomps grapes in the vat.
- **Sitting Room** — chairs and benches facing west/south windows.
  Exits: N -> Vaulted Hall (west), door. DEAD END.
- **Vaulted Hall, eastern end** — Exits: N -> Classroom; E ->
  Meditation Room; W -> Vaulted Hall (west); S -> Library.
- **Classroom** — tiered desks descending to a stage; superb
  acoustics; a large chalk board. Exits: S -> Vaulted Hall (east).
  Five student acolytes sit here under the instructor.
- **Library** — tall N-S shelves, high desks at the rear.
  Exits: N -> Vaulted Hall (east). Five adept scribes; the librarian.
- **Meditation Room** — bare walls, stained-glass skylight, mats;
  an ivory censer. Exits: W -> Vaulted Hall (east). DEAD END.

## West monastery building (via the door in Cloister west seg.2)

- **Hall, middle** — unadorned N-S hall; food smell from W, snoring
  from N. Exits: N -> Hall (northern end); E -> Cloister (door);
  W -> Dining Room (door); S -> Hall (southern end).
- **Dining Room** — long E-W tables, sconces. Exits: E -> Hall
  (middle, door); S -> Kitchen.
- **Kitchen** — great hearth, work tables, a large cabinet.
  Exits: N -> Dining Room. DEAD END. A monk cooks here.
- **Hall, northern end** — Exits: W -> Quarters (door); S -> Hall (middle).
- **Quarters** — bunk beds on all four walls, tall cabinets; the
  monks' sleeping room (snoring is ambient only — empty when visited).
  Exits: E -> Hall (north, door). DEAD END.
- **Hall, southern end** — Exits: N -> Hall (middle); W -> Templar
  gym. (No east exit — the hall's south end does not touch the cloister.)
- **Templar gym** — training dummies, mirrors, a weaponrack of
  training weapons, scuffed floor. Exits: E -> Hall (south). DEAD END.
  A templar-in-training shouts "PROJECT THE INNOCENT! BANANAS!!
  CASH!!!" — flavor, presumably.
