## Rooms

### The Forest of Tharen (field) — entrance
Sign reads: "A WARNING TO ALL EVIL-DOERS WHO WOULD ENTER THAREN FOREST!
YOU ARE UNWELCOME HERE AND WE ASK YOU KINDLY TO CONDUCT YOUR AFFAIRS
ELSEWHERE. — The inhabitants of Tharen Forest"
Exits: North (Forest of Tharen), West (dark).

### The Forest of Tharen (field) — north of entrance
Grig here (dancing, singing). **Consider: "lower level than you / you
would probably die" — consider LIES or special mob. Do NOT engage.**
Exits: East (dark), South (dark).

### The Forest of Tharen (field) — path split
Path splits north and east.
Exits: North (dark), East (dark), West (Forest of Tharen).

### The magical clearing
North of tower entrance. Leads to the Rip.
Exits: South (Entrance to the tower), others unmapped.

### The Rip in Reality (water)
"Something extremely wrong here... floating in nothingness."
**SHORTCUT: Down exits to the Temple of Midgaard.**
Exits: North (Rip), East (Rip), West (Entrance to the tower),
South (dark), Up (dark), Down (Temple of Midgaard).

### Entrance to the tower
Arched entryway, stones too large for man-like creatures. Door hanging
on hinges to the south.
Mobs: sabre-toothed chicken, young gnoblin x3, grig.
Exits: North (magical clearing), East (dark), South (Hallway of Statues).

### Hallway of Statues (north section)
Long hallway, tall statues on each wall. Stairway up between statues
to the west. Continues south; exit tower to the north.
Mobs: rabbit-dog.
Exits: North (Entrance to the tower), West (Grand Staircase),
South (Hallway of Statues).

### Hallway of Statues (south section)
More statues; small room between statues to the east (unentered).
Exits: North (Hallway of Statues), South (?), East (small room).

### The Grand Staircase
Large white stone staircase winding up into the tower to the west.
Exits: East (Hallway of Statues), West (On the Grand Staircase).

### On the Grand Staircase (3 segments, winding up)
Segment 1: stairs down east, up south. Segment 2: stairs down north,
up east. Segment 3: stairs down west, landing to the north.
**Rat-bats are aggressive here** — a large rat-bat ambushed at L8 and
took the bot 87→32 HP before noflee fled. Consider: "very tough battle."
Mobs: large rat-bat (aggressive).

### The Landing (floor 2)
Landing for the large staircase. Stairs up west, down south.
East: The entrance (dark hallway).
Mobs: rabbit-dog.
Exits: East (The entrance), West (On the Grand Staircase),
South (On the Grand Staircase).

### The entrance (floor 2)
Dark spooky hallway. Stairway west, hallway east.
Exits: East (Outside a door), West (The Landing).

### Outside a door (floor 2)
Large strong door to the south with a huge iron lock. Slit with metal
slide for observing inside safely.
**A large flesh golem guards here. Consider: "higher level than you /
you envision your entrails spread about the room." DO NOT ENGAGE.**
The HUGE KEY from the spiral likely fits this lock — untested.
Exits: West (The entrance), South (door, locked).

### The Landing (floor 3)
Another landing. Door in the south wall above the stairs (unopened).
Stairs up west, down south. Exit east.
Exits: East (The spiral), West (On the Grand Staircase),
South (On the Grand Staircase).

### The spiral (floor 3, loops around the tower)
Well-lit white-stone hallway curving around the tower exterior.
Multiple segments; windows with light.
One segment: **HUGE KEY on the ground** (taken session 77).
Same segment: locked door to the east (untried with the key).
Stone statues standing guard (non-aggressive).
Exits: loops East/West/North/South between segments; West (The Landing).

## Mobs
- Grig (forest): consider lies — "lower level" but "you would probably die." AVOID.
- Large rat-bat (staircase): aggressive, "very tough" at L8. CAUTION.
- Large flesh golem (floor-2 door): "higher level / entrails." DO NOT ENGAGE.
- Sabre-toothed chicken, young gnoblin, wild cat, rabbit-dog: huntable.
- Stone statues (spiral): non-aggressive.

## Treasure
- HUGE KEY (spiral, floor 3) — fits the golem door's huge iron lock. Untested.
- Locked door east in the spiral — unopened.

## Notes
- Tower is L1 recommended but the golem is far above that; the
  recommendation likely covers the entry/chicken/gnoblin level only.
- The Rip Down → Temple shortcut makes this a fast-travel hub:
  Tharen → Rip → Temple → anywhere.
- Session 77 kills (all Tharen Forest/tower entry): 2 wild cat,
  1 young gnoblin, 2 sabre-toothed chicken, 1 rabbit-dog (+238 XP).
