## Rooms (session 28 — mapping essentially complete)

Same room names repeat — map by identity and verify with `look` /
`exits`, per the non-reciprocal-exits rule. The village is one long
north–south slick-path spine with side branches:
- **South spine:** hut mossy paths (W) ↔ slick paths ↔ At the well
  (dead end, S). A short mossy path (E off the well slick) → hut N.
  A second mossy pair west of the well slick → hut N (distinct hut).
- **Mid spine:** two slick huts (E door, W door) + hill turnoff (E).
- **Pond branch:** gated slick (W gate) → shallow-pond grid
  (8+ "In a shallow pond" rooms, W–E/N–S interconnected) →
  On a wooden dock (far W, fishernewt, fixed brass lantern).
- **Cricket pen:** gated slick (E gate) → In the cricket pen
  (giant cricket; do not enter to fight).
- **Hill branch:** slick (E) → On a grassy hill ×2 → On a grassy hill
  (smokehouse N, dead end) → On a grassy slope ×2 → Atop a grassy
  hill → W: On a grassy hill → N: On a grassy knoll → N door:
  In a hut (newt gypsy, herbal hut, glowing — peaceful). Atop S:
  On a grassy hill → E door: In a hut (recluse hut).
- **North spine:** A wooden bridge → A cobblestone road →
  At the end of a cobblestoned road (city boundary).

Huts are all "In a hut" with peaceful newt residents (farmers,
peasants, acolytes, gypsy). No huntable mobs in the city; in the
village only mosquitoes are prey (none seen session 28).
