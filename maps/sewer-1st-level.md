# Sewer - 1st Level

**Recommended level L1** (per `where`: "Sewer - 1st level created by
DIKU. This area is recommended for players level L1"). Mapped 2026-10-02
(session 30) on Sin's live order. **This is NOT the Midgaard Storm Drain**
(a different zone, still off-limits) — this is the DIKU sewer below the
Dump.

**How to get there:** The Dump (field, south of Midgaard) → `down` the
vertical pipe → The quadruple junction under the dump. Bring a light
(`light lamp`); the whole zone is dark.

## Warnings

- **Exits may not reciprocate** (Sin's warning, confirmed live) — going
  north then back south does not always return you. Map by room
  identity, not assumed reversibility.
- **The round room rotates.** It cut off the exit back to the Dump pipe
  mid-session and trapped the mapper south of it. Treat it as a
  one-way hazard, not a landmark.
- Exit destinations read **"(too dark to tell)"** even with a lamp lit —
  the MUD hides where dark exits lead here. All links below are from
  the walk trace, not from exit listings.
- Mobs: small spiders (one fought a L5 down to 26 HP — weak, but they
  add up).

## Rooms (20 mapped)

Entry chain and branches from the 2026-10-02 walk trace (50 moves):

- **The quadruple junction under the dump** — entry room. Exits:
  N/E/S/W (dark), Up → The Dump.
- **A triple junction** — north of the above. Exits: E/S/W (dark).
- **A bend in the sewer pipe (field)** — north of triple junction.
  Exits: W/S (dark).
- **The sewer junction** — west of the bend. Exits: N/E/W (dark).
- **A junction** — north of sewer junction. Exits: N/W/S (dark).
- **Another intersection** — north of the junction. Exits: N/E/S (dark).
- **The round room** — east of triple junction area. **ROTATES —
  trapped the mapper.** Exits: E (dark).
- **A small room** — east of the round room. Exits: E/S (dark).
- **The Sewer pipe** — south of small room. Exits: N/S (dark).
- **The Grand Sewer** — south of the Sewer pipe; central hub of the
  south half. Exits: N/E/S (dark).
- **The south end of the grand pipe** — south of the Grand Sewer.
  Exits: N, W → **The pool in the sewer (door)** (seen, never entered).
- **The edge of the watery sewer (forest)** — west of the small-room
  area. Exits: E/W (dark).
- **The dark hallway** — east of the Grand Sewer. Exits: W/S (dark).
- **The dark passageway** — south of the dark hallway; a long winding
  stretch (many moves here). Exits: N/S (dark), W (dark, one link back
  to a triple junction).
- **The junction going three ways** — west of a triple junction.
  Exits: N/E/W (dark).
- **The sewer entrance** — north of the three-ways junction. Exits:
  N/S (dark).
- **The shaft** — north of the sewer entrance. Exits: S, **Down (dark,
  unexplored — likely deeper)**.
- **The sewer pipe bend (field)** — seen in the room list; exact links
  unverified.
- **A watery sewer bend (mountain)** — seen in the room list; exact
  links unverified.
